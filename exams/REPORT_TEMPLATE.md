# Capstone report template

Use this structure for research reports and for the reports that accompany application projects. Adapt the section titles when the
capstone page asks for a different structure, for example a funding template.

**Title.** A specific title that states the question or the system.

**Abstract.** 150 to 250 words: question, approach, main result and implication.

**1. Introduction.** The problem, why it matters, the research question and the contribution of the report.

**2. Background and related work.** The concepts and positions from the course and the literature that frame the question.

**3. Method.** For research reports: the structure of the argument, the sources and how they were selected. For application projects: the
design, data, implementation and evaluation protocol, with enough detail for another student to reproduce the results.

**4. Results or analysis.** The findings, with figures and tables that are referred to in the text.

**5. Discussion.** Interpretation, limitations, threats to validity and ethical considerations.

**6. Conclusion.** The answer to the question and what remains open.

**References.** Every source cited in square brackets, for example [3] or [4, 5], with complete bibliographic details.

**Appendix.** Links to the Colab notebook or repository, and a short statement on the use of generative AI tools, naming each tool and what it was used for.
